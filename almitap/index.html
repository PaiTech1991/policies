AlmiTap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Pai-Tech

About this policy

This privacy policy explains how the AlmiTap app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Pai-Tech.

Who the app is for

The app is designed for young children and is operated by a parent or another adult. The adult sets it up in the parent area and chooses the videos. Recommended from 24 months.

Information we collect or share

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any information, personal or otherwise.

  • The app has no internet access (it does not request the network permission).
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDK that sends information.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone calls.

Information stored only on your device

The following stays on the device, inside the app's private storage. It never leaves the device, and Pai-Tech has no access to it:

  • The videos you choose. The app copies them into its private storage, so they keep playing even if the original is moved.
  • Your settings: a user name (optional), the child's age (optional), time limits, session names and the playback state.
  • A short diagnostic log of app events, with no personal details.
  • Technical details about the device (model, Android version, screen size), read only to show the "Device check" screen.

If you choose to copy the device check summary or the diagnostic log, it goes to your device's clipboard. The app itself never sends it.

How to delete the information

Uninstall the app, or open the device's Settings → Apps → AlmiTap → Storage → Clear data. This deletes the copied videos and all settings. The original videos in your gallery are not affected.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information from children or from adults, and does not use any advertising identifier.

Security

The information is kept in the app's private storage, which Android separates from other apps.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
