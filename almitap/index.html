AlmiTap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026
Pai-Tech

About this policy

This privacy policy explains how the AlmiTap app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Pai-Tech.

Who the app is for

The app is intended for toddlers and young children and is operated by a parent or another adult. The adult sets the limits in the parent area and chooses the videos. Recommended from 24 months.

No information is collected or shared

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any information, personal or otherwise.

  • The app has no internet access and does not request the network permission.
  • No ads, no analytics and no third-party libraries that send information.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone calls.

The "Privacy policy" button in the parent area opens this address in the device's browser. The app itself does not connect to the network.

Information stored only on your mobile device

The details are stored only in the app's private storage, and the app does not send them anywhere.

Pai-Tech has no access to any of the following information:

  • Videos: the app copies them into its private storage so they keep playing, even if the original was moved.
  • Settings: user name (optional), the child's age (optional), time limits, session names and the playback state, stored only on the mobile device.
  • A diagnostic log of app events (with the session number only, no names or content).
  • Technical details about the device (model, Android version, screen size), read only to show the "Device check" screen.
  • If you choose to copy the device check summary or the diagnostic log, it is copied only to the device's clipboard. The app itself never sends it.

How to delete the information

Uninstall the app, or open the device's Settings → Apps → AlmiTap → Storage → Clear data. Note that this also deletes the copies of the videos, together with all the settings. The original videos in the gallery are not affected.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information from children or from adults, and does not use an advertising identifier.

Security

The information is stored in the app's private storage, which the operating system keeps separate from other apps.

Changes to this policy

If and when this privacy policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a "last updated" date.
